MIG slices: conservative capacity estimate, and proof they carry real work

Dispatching against the nominal slice size oversold the hardware: a 3g.20gb slice measured
19.6 GB total but only 11.0 GB actually free, so the first two jobs sent there OOM'd and
fell back to a full card (the OOM-retry path doing exactly its job, but at the cost of a
wasted attempt). The estimate is now 55% of the profile, which reproduces the measurement
(3g.20gb -> 11.3 GB, 1g.5gb -> 2.8 GB). It is an estimate, not a measurement, and the code
says so; OOM retry stays the backstop.

Also lowered the queue's min_free thresholds so a task can be placed on a 20 GB slice when
it actually fits, instead of demanding a whole card: 18 GB for the long-sequence tasks and
10 GB otherwise. And te_human moves back to batch 1 -- at batch 2 the quadratic attention
pushes it to ~20 GB, which excludes the very slices we just unlocked.

Cosmetic: the dispatch line no longer prints "gpu" in front of a MIG UUID.
