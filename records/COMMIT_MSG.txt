Analysis tooling and two correctness gates for the published table

- eval/make_tables.py: head-to-head table with paired bootstrap CIs, Wilcoxon
  signed-rank and Benjamini-Hochberg correction, and a **protocol-conformity gate**.
  The auto-dispatcher halves the batch size when a shared card runs out of memory --
  the right engineering call, but a protocol deviation that still writes a
  result.json, so without this gate such a run would quietly enter the published
  table with a different effective batch. Conforming runs are now verified against
  the registry (batch, epochs, max_len, lr) and deviating ones are listed and
  excluded rather than averaged in.
- eval/factor_probe.py: the Fig.5 probe. Region-labelled sequence pools, factor
  coordinates from the learned basis, linear probe plus ARI/FMI, permutation test and
  BH correction. The control is a *random-subspace* control rather than a rotated
  basis, because a linear probe is rotation-invariant and rotating P_k would prove
  nothing; what needs controlling for is whether the learned r-dimensional subspaces
  beat arbitrary subspaces of the same rank. If nothing survives FDR, claim C3 is
  withdrawn and Fig.5 becomes a dynamics panel, as the proposal allows.
- eval/plot_dynamics.py: Fig.6 panels from train_log.jsonl (losses, the four collapse
  monitors, curriculum and realised mask fraction), plus dynamics.csv.
- run_meta.json is now written atomically: the OOM retry starts a fresh process while
  the previous one may still be finishing, and two interleaved writers produced a
  truncated file that the report failed to parse. queue_matrix.py gained --force to
  re-run a deviated cell in place.
- monitor.py: stall threshold raised to 45 minutes; the old 25-minute value flagged
  long jobs during their epoch-boundary evaluation.
