Mechanism: where each design choice pays off
Freeze the backbone
Fine-tuning 650M params on ~14k structures overfits family quirks; a frozen LM keeps the 36M-sequence representation, so the head learns "decide", not "memorise". Same corpus, decontaminated: 6/8 references cleared with 70× fewer trained params.
Flat 2D head, not a deep stack
Pair scoring is a local-on-pairs problem once embeddings are good. A shallow 2D residual head trains to convergence without overfitting short benchmarks and decodes in 2.9 ms — the 124.78 seq/s result is architectural, not merely engineering.
Priors at decode, not in the loss
Turner energies and the non-crossing constraint act on the probability map, so OOD splits inherit physics even when training families are absent (+0.37/+0.25). The ensemble then only has to average two family heads — precision + recall — to take 7/8.
